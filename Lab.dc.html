Collaborative Human-AI Learning Ecologies Lab

The CoHALE Lab studies how learners, teachers, and AI systems think and work together across STEM and computing contexts. We design AI-mentored learning environments, study them in real classrooms with partner teachers, and build theory about what productive human-AI collaboration looks like in education.

STEM+C learning
AI-guided, authentic scientific inquiry in K-12 classrooms, from agricultural science to XR-enhanced maker experiences.
Computational literacies & participation
Disciplinary computational thinking, data practices, and equitable participation, with a focus on rural schools.
AI literacy
AI literacy and AI-TPACK for preservice and in-service teachers, and safeguards for responsible AI integration.
