Featured · NSF CAREER Award · PI
3AforCT: AI-Guided, Authentic, Ag-STEM Inquiry for Cultivating Computational Thinking in Rural Middle Schools
A five-year program (2026–2031) in which rural middle school students instrument school gardens with environmental sensors, take on collaborative professional roles, and work with a classroom-safe AI peer mentor that scaffolds data-to-decision reasoning. The project produces an agriculture-specific computational thinking taxonomy and open, adoption-ready curriculum materials.
